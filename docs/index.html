How to read this

Independent numbers come from BC government automated snow-pillow stations (hourly, satellite-transmitted) and Avalanche Canada forecasts. Stations are usually off the ski runs, sometimes tens of kilometres away and at different elevations, so use them for trend and to sanity-check the resort. Depth change is not the same as snowfall: new snow settles, so a station gaining 8 cm can match 12–15 cm of reported new snow. "Resort higher" only appears when the gap is larger than settling explains. Resort numbers are read from each resort's own page and shown separately, never blended in.
